This is a reviewable execution plan, not a record of passing infrastructure tests. No production application, credentials, volumes or connected accounts may be used. Large fleet provisioning is outside this bounded run.

Reconciled against source `8da5c8f` on 2026-11-19. Live infrastructure, provider credentials and qualification results were not re-verified for this revision. Use the [staging runbook](../infra/platform/staging-bootstrap.md) for role/image setup, subject to the unresolved bootstrap contract below.

A successful schema-owner migration or database attachment does not grant a distinct serving login access. Grant current and future table/sequence privileges under the actual migration owner and verify using the same restricted serving connection.

For KMS, an administrator's same-tenant decrypt/different-context refusal proves envelope mechanics only. Run the [restricted runtime-principal check](../infra/platform/staging-bootstrap.md#0b-aws-key-service-and-backup-bucket-about-10-minutes-of-the-owners-time-then-one-command) with the actual credentials to be imported. Require positive generate/decrypt and an explicit wrong-purpose `AccessDeniedException`; the current script's `ok:true` or exit zero alone is insufficient. Neither local mocks nor an administrator identity qualify runtime IAM.

This document does not supply that authority. Do not seed evidence, reuse another image's approvals, disable gate checks or manufacture tenants directly in the directory. Steps below that need tenant inventory remain blocked until that contract is resolved. Image construction and the credential-free, non-tenant containment job can be prepared independently; they do not resolve the dependency.

## Bounded execution

1. Finish deterministic implementation tests and migrations locally. Run real local PostgreSQL identity/session/dispatch tests. Record pass/fail/skip separately; do not report fixtures as live-provider validation.
2. Build, scan and sign one immutable candidate using the [release workflow](../infra/tenant-host/README.md). Use the [real-Fly qualification job](../infra/tenant-host/qualification/README.md) on that exact published host digest and its published reports/images.json. It binds reviewed source and installed runtime bytes, runs the constrained container suite and installed preflight, and checks Chrome sandboxing plus positive/negative firewall routes. Require util-linux `mount`/`umount` with `--no-canonicalize`, Docker support for `bind-recursive=disabled`, and working private nonrecursive read-only binds. Include selected-folder writes, path replacement during mounting, nested-mount exposure and forbidden host/file/socket reads; separately cover CLI credentials, browser profiles, database/foreign MCP ownership, folder revocation during handshake/active work and failed removal shutdown in their applicable suites. Archive which evidence covers each boundary. The containment job's synthetic companion route does not prove real app authorization or browser health. Any unauthorized success stops the run; do not relax the policy to pass.
3. After the bootstrap contract is resolved, start the approved tenant inventory with synthetic data only. Check manifest/DB/volume mismatch fails startup; inspect listeners and firewall behavior including IPv6 and metadata destinations. Begin workload measurement with one runner: app + desktop + one runner ceilings total `1536 + 1536 + 768 = 3840 MiB`, leaving only 256 MiB on the initial 4096 MiB guest before host/kernel/Docker overhead. Two runners already total 4608 MiB. Collect trusted fresh usage and qualify the [capacity-maintenance path](tenant-capacity-operations.md) before the reviewed move to the 8 GiB staging target. Expand toward one active bot, up to three windows and the eight-helper concurrency target only after sizing/headroom review; eight simultaneous runner/MCP containers total 9216 MiB with app/desktop, exceeding even the 8192 MiB guest by 1024 MiB before host overhead. These ceilings are not reservations or evidence of safe actual usage. Measure OOM/resource behavior; do not add account queues or helper caps to hide failures.
4. Exercise a fixed batch of at most 100 synthetic jobs over 30 minutes: login rotation/revocation, queued tool revocation, viewer watch/control substitution, terminal ownership, runtime renewal/lost responses, worker restart and uncertain external-write reconciliation. Verify initial hold, exact-revision readiness, stop/wake, expired refresh replacement and config-file bootstrap replay. In a separately bounded revocation test, connect test clients to explicitly selected platform brokers: revoke sessions/native pairings on one and verify the other closes its relays; terminate the LISTEN backend and confirm health 503, relay closure and fresh authorization after reconnect. This tests revocation propagation only. Keep exactly one normally serving platform process: TunnelBroker's tenant tunnel map is process-local, so two replicas are not safe shared routing or failover. Verify delegated tenant cookies cannot authenticate any platform account/admin endpoint. Delay cloud app/volume/machine creation until after deletion starts; cleanup must remain pending until the resource outcome is accounted for. Use a controlled upstream fixture for destructive/error scenarios.
5. Add controlled provider credentials only after containment passes. Perform at most 20 read-only provider calls per account and 5 explicit test writes to disposable records. Check another owner cannot select a foreign account and one owner with two accounts always reaches the chosen account. Check MCP SSRF/rebinding/redirect and response bounds against controlled fixtures, not arbitrary networks.
6. Exercise the [isolated recovery/update commands](tenant-recovery-operations.md) with synthetic encrypted backups: foreign host/runtime JWTs must fail; disconnect during download, generation replacement, damaged parts, missing published data and lost completion responses must prevent unsafe activation. Verify source stop/removal, pre-activation rollback, refusal of stale-volume rollback after any run permit, snapshot-era job/message reconciliation and disabled recovered MCP/Mac consent. Confirm old-volume retention never deletes a current or attached volume and waits for actual provider deletion. Separately, once concrete legacy source/target adapters exist, migrate synthetic records with proven credential/file ownership and one authoritative writer. Measure database RPO <=5 minutes, profile age <=24 hours and full restore <=30 minutes, including disk space for encrypted and restored copies. Inject storage/deletion failures and verify durable retry visibility.
7. Archive metrics, image/kernel versions and sanitized findings; remove test credentials, test records, temporary images/VMs/volumes as agreed in the inventory. No automatic deletion outside that inventory.

Single-host testing cannot establish cross-microVM/private-network isolation. After it passes, a separately bounded two-tenant Fly test with two isolated applications/volumes/networks must verify tenant A, including compromised workload root, cannot reach tenant B or use B's runtime/provider/viewer identity. Do not mark that gate passed from shared-host simulation.
